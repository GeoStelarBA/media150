Creative Statement

I’m a person that enjoys games to the point it’s an obsession, not just for how they play but also for how they look. I’ve always wanted to know what makes certain games stand out visually compared to others in their respective genre. I want to be able to make art similar to games I like, but not simply just said games as a framework. My interest lies in understanding what makes a design work, and using some of the techniques I plan to hopefully learn from college, and create something myself or even with some friends of mine.

My inspirations come from various games, anime, drawings, and so on with some examples including, but not limited to: Sonic the Hedgehog, Mega Man, Shin Megami Tensei/Persona, Pokemon, Dragon Ball. Each one of these examples are stylized in certain ways, whether it be through their varying environments or features in character design such as color theory used for a certain character or the type of clothes a character would tend to wear. Visuals aren’t enough to tell a story, as music or sound in general also do a lot of work in sending a message. Whether it be a fusion of styles such as Pop Fusion or something standalone like Jazz or Electronic or something melodic but fundamentally unstructured, I want to find out what exactly appeals to me at the moment.
